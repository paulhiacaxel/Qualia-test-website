# Qualia: repeat bookings, insurance and launch decisions

Research checked **11 October 2026**. This is a decision brief for the owner, broker and Florida counsel, not a coverage determination or ready-to-use legal terms. No providers were contacted and nothing was purchased.

## Keep bookings on Qualia by making repeat sessions useful

These are documented business models, not evidence that a particular incentive prevents leakage.

| Official benchmark | Current published model | Useful lesson for Qualia |
|---|---|---|
| [Preply commission model](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4171383-preply-commission-model) | Free profile; 100% commission on a new student's trial; subsequent commission declines from 33% to 18% with accumulated teaching hours. | Reward continued use. Do not copy its unpaid-trial economics for an experience marketplace. |
| [Wyzant tutor fees](https://support.wyzant.com/tutors/tutor-payments/what-is-the-fee-structure-for-tutors-listed-on-wyzant/) | 25% tutor fee normally; no tutor fee for eligible students the tutor refers. A 9% student service fee remains. | Distinguish new business Qualia acquires from clients a host brings. “No host commission” does not mean no payment or guest fees. |
| [Lessonface teaching](https://www.lessonface.com/about-teaching) | 15% for platform-sourced private students; 5% for teacher-sourced students. Scheduling, messaging, lesson notes/files and cancellation administration accompany booking. | A smaller continuing fee can pay for useful administration; repeat bookings need continuing value. |

[Wyzant's payment policies](https://support.wyzant.com/tutors/tutor-payments/tutor-payment-policies/) tie payment protection to eligibility and records, including payment information, submission deadlines and disclosed cancellation terms. They require platform payment for students retained through Wyzant; that is not proof of blanket exclusivity over a teacher's business. Check each prospective host's actual obligations rather than assuming platform membership disqualifies them.

**Recommended Qualia sequence:**

1. MVP: clear booking status, payment receipt, cancellation/refund terms, calendar invitations, reminders, a booking-linked conversation and a simple rebook button. Give hosts payout status and a booking history they can reconcile. Do not promise guaranteed payouts or insurance as a retention benefit.
2. After initial bookings: saved guest preferences with consent, repeat-session suggestions and completed-booking reviews. Measure second-booking conversion, host earnings and support burden before adding memberships or packages.
3. Consider a lower rate for host-introduced customers only after approving unit economics. No new rate is committed here. If adopting an anti-circumvention term, counsel should define its scope and duration for Qualia introductions; it should not imply ownership of every host customer.

Honor the requested **no commission percentage on the public homepage**. “Free to list” can describe the listing fee. Before a host accepts terms or publishes, show their actual commission, processing-fee allocation, payout timing and founding offer in plain language; show guests their complete total before payment.

Track the founding incentive in the host dashboard: completed eligible bookings remaining, end date and later rate. Owner decisions remain: standard rate; lifetime founding rate; when six months starts; treatment of cancellations/refunds; and the number, expiry and stacking rules for referral credits. Say precisely which charge is waived. Foregone commission is `eligible booking value × otherwise-applicable rate`; processing, refunds, support and acquisition still cost money. Avoid uncapped referral promises until those rules are approved.

## Insurance and waivers: questions to resolve before taking bookings

Get written broker advice separately for **Qualia's marketplace operations** and **each host's actual paid activity**. [Florida DFS](https://myfloridacfo.com/division/consumers/understanding-insurance/commercial-property-insurance) explains that forms, exclusions and endorsements vary, and professional and cyber liability may be separate additions. A generic business policy name is not evidence that instruction is covered.

Ask the broker: which entities and activities are insured; whether host negligence and Qualia's own alleged negligence are addressed; which participant-injury, professional-services, watercraft, food, home-business or abuse exclusions apply; whether off-site venues are included; and what limits, deductibles, endorsements and claims-reporting duties fit the launch. What host evidence must Qualia collect, and is additional-insured status appropriate? Review the policy/endorsements needed to answer those questions, not just a certificate label. Obtain quotes before allocating the remaining $1–5k budget; no reliable premium for this mixed marketplace has been established.

| Activity | Listing review and broker/counsel questions |
|---|---|
| Language, music, ordinary photography and low-hazard art | Is paid instruction covered at this specific home/studio/public venue? Check permission, equipment risks and emergency arrangements. Keep image/publicity consent separate from injury acknowledgments. |
| Fitness | Does coverage address instruction-related injury as well as premises accidents? Review qualifications, participant suitability process, touch consent and emergency plan. Avoid storing detailed health histories in ordinary booking messages. |
| Cooking | Confirm food preparation/storage location, equipment, allergies, knives/heat and any alcohol. Ask the regulator which licensing regime covers the actual class. DBPR distinguishes catering from a narrowly described personal-chef situation; do not assume every cooking lesson qualifies for an exemption. [DBPR guide](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/hr/forms/documents/5030_037.pdf), November 2020, checked on the date above. |
| Art using kilns, blades, solvents or other hazardous equipment | Review the specific tools, ventilation, protective equipment, participant limits and insurer acceptance; the category name alone is insufficient. |
| Sailing | Require specialist review of the vessel, operator credentials, passenger/instruction model, commercial marine coverage, safety equipment and weather cancellation plan. FWC says for-hire boats on federally defined navigable waters require a Coast Guard captain's license and special equipment. [FWC guidance](https://myfwc.com/viewing/outfitters-guides/). Do not automatically apply rental/livery rules to a captain-led lesson or assume a personal boat policy covers it. |

Waivers and insurance serve different functions ([ERGO NEXT explainer](https://www.nextinsurance.com/blog/personal-trainer-waiver-form/), 22 April 2024). Neither the presence of a waiver nor an uploaded policy establishes that a particular claim will be covered or defeated. Have Florida counsel review adult waivers, electronic acceptance, risk disclosures, liability allocation, cancellations and incident handling for the actual activities. Do not copy an internet waiver into production.

**Minors need a separate decision.** [Florida Statutes §744.301(3)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0744/Sections/0744.301.html) specifically addresses natural-guardian releases for inherent risks of commercial activities and prescribes notice formatting. A generic adult checkbox does not resolve this. An adults-only initial pilot is a proposed simplification, subject to owner approval; if minors are included, resolve guardian authority, safeguarding and insurer requirements first.

Confirm venue rules municipality by municipality. [Miami business licensing](https://www.miami.gov/Business-Licenses/Business-Licensing) covers local licensing routes. [Miami Beach COFA](https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/city-hall/parks-and-recreation/cofa/) describes paid organized park activities with five or more participants; it does **not** settle every one-on-one park-use question. Ask about the actual venue/activity rather than treating that threshold as blanket permission.

## Proposed launch gate

Start with approved activities and venues whose insurance and operating requirements have been resolved; sailing and other higher-hazard listings can remain pending specialist review. In the existing safety review → optional consultation → live flow, record category-specific evidence, reviewer, decision, expiry dates and outstanding conditions. An optional call cannot substitute for missing required evidence. Stripe identity checks do not complete this review.

Store accepted terms/waiver version, signer, booking and timestamp with restricted access. Define what triggers a new acceptance, document-expiry suspension or incident escalation. Public copy should describe the review accurately and make no blanket “fully insured” or safety guarantee.

**Owner approvals needed:** fee and incentive rules; adults-only versus minors; initial activity scope; legal/broker spending allocation; and approved host terms/waiver process. All are unresolved decisions, not changes already made to Qualia.

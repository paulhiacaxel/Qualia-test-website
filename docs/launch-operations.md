## Keep bookings on Qualia by making repeat sessions useful

These are documented business models, not evidence that a particular incentive prevents leakage.

Do not copy its unpaid-trial economics for an experience marketplace.                                       |

[Wyzant's payment policies](https://support.wyzant.com/tutors/tutor-payments/tutor-payment-policies/) tie payment protection to eligibility and records, including payment information, submission deadlines and disclosed cancellation terms. They require platform payment for students retained through Wyzant; that is not proof of blanket exclusivity over a teacher's business. Check each prospective host's actual obligations rather than assuming platform membership disqualifies them.

**Recommended Qualia sequence:**

1. MVP: clear booking status, payment receipt, cancellation/refund terms, calendar invitations, reminders, a booking-linked conversation and a simple rebook button. Give hosts payout status and a booking history they can reconcile. Do not promise guaranteed payouts or insurance as a retention benefit.
2. After initial bookings: saved guest preferences with consent, repeat-session suggestions and completed-booking reviews. Measure second-booking conversion, host earnings and support burden before adding memberships or packages.
3. Consider a lower rate for host-introduced customers only after approving unit economics. No new rate is committed here. If adopting an anti-circumvention term, counsel should define its scope and duration for Qualia introductions; it should not imply ownership of every host customer.

Honor the requested **no commission percentage on the public homepage**. “Free to list” can describe the listing fee. Before a host accepts terms or publishes, show their actual commission, processing-fee allocation, payout timing and founding offer in plain language; show guests their complete total before payment.

Track each host’s frozen founding terms: eligible paid bookings remaining, calendar-month end date and ongoing rate. The owner approved the [revised launch decisions](launch-decisions-proposed.md), including rates, free-booking count, duration, cohort cap, refund restoration and referral limits. Save them as a versioned policy through the authorized configuration flow; approval alone is not activation. Foregone commission is `eligible booking value × otherwise-applicable rate`; it is not cash spending. Actual processor deductions do not cover Qualia’s separate Connect overhead.

## Insurance and waivers: questions to resolve before taking bookings

Get written broker advice separately for **Qualia's marketplace operations** and **each host's actual paid activity**. [Florida DFS](https://myfloridacfo.com/division/consumers/understanding-insurance/commercial-property-insurance) explains that forms, exclusions and endorsements vary, and professional and cyber liability may be separate additions. A generic business policy name is not evidence that instruction is covered.

Ask the broker: which entities and activities are insured; whether host negligence and Qualia's own alleged negligence are addressed; which participant-injury, professional-services, watercraft, food, home-business or abuse exclusions apply; whether off-site venues are included; and what limits, deductibles, endorsements and claims-reporting duties fit the launch. What host evidence must Qualia collect, and is additional-insured status appropriate? Review the policy/endorsements needed to answer those questions, not just a certificate label. Obtain quotes before allocating the remaining $1–5k budget; no reliable premium for this mixed marketplace has been established.

Waivers and insurance serve different functions ([ERGO NEXT explainer](https://www.nextinsurance.com/blog/personal-trainer-waiver-form/), 22 April 2024). Neither the presence of a waiver nor an uploaded policy establishes that a particular claim will be covered or defeated. Have Florida counsel review adult waivers, electronic acceptance, risk disclosures, liability allocation, cancellations and incident handling for the actual activities. Do not copy an internet waiver into production.

## Proposed launch gate

Start with approved activities and venues whose insurance and operating requirements have been resolved; sailing and other higher-hazard listings can remain pending specialist review. In the existing safety review → optional consultation → live flow, record category-specific evidence, reviewer, decision, expiry dates and outstanding conditions. An optional call cannot substitute for missing required evidence. Stripe identity checks do not complete this review.

Store accepted terms/waiver version, signer, booking and timestamp with restricted access. Define what triggers a new acceptance, document-expiry suspension or incident escalation. Public copy should describe the review accurately and make no blanket “fully insured” or safety guarantee.

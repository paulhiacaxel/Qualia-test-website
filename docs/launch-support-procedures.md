# Launch support and privacy operations

Owner approval: October 12, 2026 (America/New_York). Operator: Axel Paulhiac, trading as Qualia. Public support and privacy contact: `support@bookqualia.com`.

## Support inbox and booking issues

Axel monitors the support mailbox and the application's admin support queue. Review both each working day and before any scheduled experience. This is an operating procedure, not a published response-time guarantee. Keep an issue tied to its booking reference; never ask for passwords, card numbers, bank details or full identity documents in messages.

For an immediate threat, direct the person to emergency services. Pause an affected listing when conditions cannot safely be verified. Record the reported facts, affected bookings, action taken and reviewer. Do not mark an unsafe or disputed experience complete merely to release funds. Review any incident against the activity's approved conditions, evidence and cancellation policy.

For a refund, verify the booking, payer, captured cancellation policy, actual payment state and already refunded amount. Use the application's payment administration actions. Keep provider reconciliation active while a payment is paused. If a provider request times out, reconcile the existing operation before trying again; do not create a second charge/refund/transfer to work around an unknown result. Contact the guest with the recorded outcome, without promising an exact bank-posting date.

## Privacy requests and retention

Accept access, correction and deletion requests at the support mailbox. Verify control of the relevant account using the existing signed-in account or registered return-email channel. For a guardian request, verify authority without collecting more child information than needed. Log the request date, scope, verification, decision, legal/financial hold if any, and completion. Apply any legally required deadlines; escalate an uncertain request promptly instead of making an unsupported legal determination.

Retention is purpose-based and reviewed manually. There is no automated account or transaction deletion schedule. Keep account/profile data while needed to operate the account, and remove or de-identify information that is no longer needed after review. Preserve payment reconciliation, disputes, consent evidence, safety incidents and records subject to a legal or fraud-prevention hold for the applicable obligation. Document the reason and review point for retained records. Do not delete an Auth user before reviewing token/session revocation, related records and statutory/payment obligations.

Review pending privacy requests and outstanding holds monthly. Delete unnecessary copies of support attachments after the issue is resolved and retention needs are checked. Use the providers' supported controls and verify the result; account deletion alone does not erase all related application or provider records. Never promise immediate or universal deletion, a fixed statutory retention term that has not been checked, or deletion of records another provider must retain.

## Listing and activity evidence

Review each actual experience, venue, operator and proposed participant age range. Request concrete credentials, licensing/venue permission, insurance information and emergency/supervision plans appropriate to that experience. A completed text field is not proof that its claim is verified. Record evidence inspected, the review decision, expiry and any conditions. Keep unresolved listings unpublished; this is not a category-wide exclusion.

Where a specific activity needs an adult/guardian release, obtain the appropriate reviewed document and a separate verifiable acceptance process before approving that activity. The account agreement and participation acknowledgment are not liability releases. Optional photography/publicity permission remains separate. Do not assert that Qualia supplies insurance or background checks.

## Daily launch checks

- Review failed Stripe events, unknown operations, financial holds and unresolved refunds in the payment dashboard; reconcile before releasing earnings.
- Release only after the experience ends, the 48-hour delay has elapsed, completion is recorded and all holds are clear. Stripe transfer success is not bank-arrival confirmation.
- Check the email queue for failed or uncertain delivery and inspect provider status before retrying. Keep message IDs and recipient-safe diagnostic metadata; never log authentication tokens or full message contents.
- Watch application errors and scheduled-job results. The five-minute email job is authenticated; an empty successful queue is healthy but does not prove delivery of every lifecycle template.
- Review expiring listing evidence and pause affected inventory when approval no longer covers the whole scheduled experience.

Honor ordinary stop replies as well as the approved email unsubscribe link. Do not send transactional booking messages as prospect outreach. Existing approvals and suppression records govern recruitment; this launch configuration does not approve new recipients or repeat already-sent invitations.

# Qualia brand kit

Prepared October 12, 2026 (Miami time). Review draft; these files do not create social accounts or publish posts. Preview: [brand-launch-preview.html](brand-launch-preview.html). Account setup, official platform research and fuller profile copy: [brand-launch-plan.md](brand-launch-plan.md).

## The direction

**One person. A whole new world.**

Qualia makes Miami feel more personal: time with someone who knows a craft, space to ask a question, and a different way to spend an afternoon. The identity should feel considered and human. It should be equally at home beside a ceramicist’s workbench, a photographer’s contact sheet or a sailing instructor’s notebook.

Keep the existing aperture and wordmark. Use a warm editorial layout, generous space, quiet rules and strong type. Moss is the recognizable anchor. Gold adds a small point of interest, rather than a luxury badge. Miami comes through people, places and specific knowledge, not a borrowed nightclub or resort aesthetic.

## Voice

Write as Axel or a small team introducing someone worth knowing. Name the particular thing a person can share. Prefer “A photographer’s way of seeing the city” to “Unlock unforgettable premium experiences.” Ask a clear, low-pressure question. Keep one main action per post.

Use **personal**, **one-on-one**, **craft**, **curiosity**, **Miami**, **meet**, **learn** and **share** when they mean something concrete. Avoid fake urgency, “Miami’s #1,” unearned exclusivity, invented waiting lists and claims of reviews or customers we do not have. “First 30 Founding Hosts” describes the cohort limit, not how many have joined or how many spots remain.

Current status language: **“Building our first Miami collection.”** When real reviewed inventory exists, change this in bios and pinned captions together. An active payment integration alone is not a reason to say experiences are available now.

Review is a process, not a promise that an activity is risk-free, insured, certified or background checked. Do not make those claims unless the specific claim is verified and approved for that particular listing.

I want it to be easier to spend time with people who really know their craft — whether that’s cooking, photography, music or something you’ve always wanted to try. Right now, I’m meeting our first hosts and helping them shape their experiences. If that’s you, I’d love to talk.

Film near a real Miami setting in daylight, with clear audio and no music that obscures the introduction. Keep the first take natural. Add accurate captions. This can replace the process post in the pinned set once ready.

## Real photography brief

Ask the first willing hosts for three useful images: an approachable portrait, hands doing the work, and the real place where the experience happens. Request written permission to feature the image and agree how to credit it. Capture specific details such as clay on a wheel, light on a contact sheet or a sailor checking a line. Only call something a Qualia session if it was one.

Do not copy photographs from lead websites or Instagram without permission. Do not turn scraped people into endorsement imagery. No fabricated guests, reviews, host headshots or synthetic “previous experiences.” Until real material is approved, these typographic templates stand on their own.

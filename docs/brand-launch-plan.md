# Qualia brand and social launch plan

Prepared October 12, 2026, Miami time. Draft copy and setup plan; no social profile, post, ad campaign or paid subscription has been created by this document.

## Positioning and visual system

**Qualia — One person. A whole new world.**

A personal introduction to Miami through the people who know a craft well. Keep the warmth and curiosity of the existing site: real people, specific skills, an afternoon that feels personal. The initial audience is prospective independent hosts and curious Miami residents. Visitors are a second audience. Early content should make people understand the idea and want to meet the first hosts.

Use the existing aperture, not a replacement logo. `public/brand_assets/qualia-avatar-1024.png` is the square master for social profiles; its rings and spokes use the same moss color. The SVG is the editable source. Check a circular crop and small mobile preview before applying it.

Photography: natural light, a person's hands or face, the tools of a craft, a recognizable local setting. Use actual host work with permission and proper credit. Until hosts consent, use brand typography, Axel's approved founder photo and original Miami footage. Do not stage fictitious completed Qualia sessions, fabricate reviews or imply stock/generated people are hosts.

The public story is **“Building our first Miami collection”** until real reviewed listings are available. Payment infrastructure being active does not mean experiences can already be booked. Avoid “Miami's leading,” member counts, booking counts, guarantees or unverified claims that hosts are insured or background checked.

## Yelp: decide eligibility before spending the credit

Yelp's own guidelines allow direct local service businesses without storefronts, but exclude online booking websites and third-party directory/referral services. **Our inference: Qualia's current intermediary marketplace model is likely ineligible**, even though its hosts offer in-person activities. A mailbox is not a customer-facing Qualia venue, and a service-area label would not change that business model. [6]

Do not submit Qualia as a chef, sailing school or other direct provider merely to obtain a category. Ask Yelp to evaluate the accurate marketplace description first. Independent hosts may separately qualify for their own listings.

The exact Anytime Mailbox coupon amount, qualification rules, minimum spend, expiration, renewal and cancellation terms have **not been verified**. The user should open the offer or provide its actual link/screenshot; read it before activating anything. Record total potential out-of-pocket cost, whether a card is required, when billing starts, and whether advertising continues after the credit runs out. A credit is not evidence of zero-cost advertising or listing eligibility. No Yelp or Meta ad budget is authorized by this plan.

## Access and final verification

Use signed-in owner access for setup. Browser access is normally more useful than a posting plugin for account creation, identity/security prompts, Page ownership and connecting Meta assets. Reuse already-existing Qualia profiles if they exist; search and inspect before creating duplicates. The exact browser/plugin availability must be verified in the active conversation.

Before calling each profile complete: open its public view, test every link, send no message merely to test contact, inspect mobile avatar/cover crops, verify the correct owner controls it, confirm recovery and 2FA, and record its permanent URL. Add only those verified URLs to Qualia's website and email signature. Do not install a scheduling service, buy verification, or start a campaign merely to finish basic setup.

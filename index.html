Trust & safety

Every experience is reviewed before it goes live.

Qualia is a marketplace, not the provider — each experience is run by an independent host. Before anything is published, our team reviews it directly.

What every listing is reviewed for

  • Safety risks — what could realistically go wrong, and whether it's been accounted for.
  • Legal & licensing requirements — the activity meets applicable laws in its location.
  • Host credibility — real experience and standing to teach what they're offering.
  • Clarity — the description says exactly what a guest can expect.
  • Fit — whether the activity belongs on Qualia at all.

What we ask of hosts

Hosts are responsible for describing their experience accurately, following applicable law, and holding any license, permit, certification, or insurance their activity requires. Higher-risk categories go through additional approval, and may need proof of insurance or certification before they're listed:

Boating Aviation Water sports Climbing Fitness & training Driving Tools & machinery Experiences with children

What you agree to as a guest

Before you book, you'll see the cancellation policy, host and platform rules, safety expectations, and how refunds and disputes are handled — in plain language, not fine print you have to go looking for.

For physical or higher-risk experiences, you'll sign a short digital waiver acknowledging the assumption of risk before you show up.

If something goes wrong

Every guest and host can report an injury, misconduct, unsafe behavior, or a misleading listing directly to our safety team — and we act on it.

Qualia carries its own liability insurance as we grow, and some experiences are declined outright when the risk to people or the platform is too high.

Report a concern →

We're not trying to remove every risk — just the ones we can see coming.

Long-term, a dedicated safety and approval team reviews listings, consults with hosts directly, and keeps our standards current.
